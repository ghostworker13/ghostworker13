## About

This account supports the engineering work of **ACK Techs**, an independent product and technology team based in Istanbul, Turkey. ACK Techs builds digital products that help people and teams make decisions and save time, combining software, AI and design, and shapes each product together with the people who will use it.

Our focus areas are **backend engineering**, **AI and LLM systems**, **autonomous agents** and **observability**.

## Principles

- Understand the problem first.
- Build small, learn fast.
- Pair function with character.
- Share successes and failures openly.
